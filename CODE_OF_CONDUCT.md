# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

[简体中文](./CODE_OF_CONDUCT.zh-CN.md)

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Demonstrating empathy and kindness toward other people
- Respecting differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Accepting responsibility, apologizing to those affected by mistakes, and learning from the experience
- Focusing on what is best for the community

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Sexualized language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing another person's private information without explicit permission
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing standards of acceptable behavior. They may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issue edits, and other contributions that do not align with this Code of Conduct, and will communicate moderation reasons when appropriate.

Maintainers who receive a report must protect the reporter's privacy, avoid conflicts of interest, and handle the report fairly. A maintainer who is involved in an incident must not decide its outcome.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces and when an individual officially represents the project in public spaces. Project spaces include repository activity, review, discussions, events, and private communication connected to project participation.

## Reporting

The organization has not yet published a verified private conduct-reporting channel. Establishing and testing a controlled private channel remains a hard prerequisite for enabling Issues or Discussions, actively soliciting public contributions, or beginning formal enforcement of this Code of Conduct. Publishing source for read-only inspection does not open those interaction channels or invite contributions.

The channel must have verified ownership, limited access, retention and handoff rules, and a tested reporter path before this gate passes. GitHub private vulnerability reporting accepts security vulnerabilities only and cannot replace a conduct-reporting channel.

Do not put sensitive conduct details, private information, or evidence in a public issue or vulnerability report. Until the controlled private channel is explicitly published, retain sensitive evidence securely and do not treat an unlisted email, chat, vulnerability form, or maintainer account as the reporting channel. No fixed response time is promised.

## Enforcement Guidelines

Maintainers will use these guidelines to determine consequences for behavior they consider unacceptable:

### 1. Correction

Impact: Inappropriate language or other behavior judged unprofessional or unwelcome.

Consequence: A private written warning explaining the violation and expected correction. A public apology may be requested when appropriate.

### 2. Warning

Impact: A violation through a single incident or a series of actions.

Consequence: A warning with conditions for continued participation. The person may be asked to avoid contact with involved people for a specified period. Violating the conditions may lead to a temporary or permanent ban.

### 3. Temporary Ban

Impact: A serious violation or sustained unacceptable behavior.

Consequence: Temporary removal from interaction or public communication with the project for a specified period. Attempts to evade the restriction may lead to a permanent ban.

### 4. Permanent Ban

Impact: A pattern of violations, harassment, aggression toward individuals, or disparagement of protected groups.

Consequence: Permanent removal from public interaction within the project community.
